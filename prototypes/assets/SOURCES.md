# Prototype logo sources

These assets are used only in the local education-section prototype.

- Shenzhen University: downloaded from the official Shenzhen University visual identity page, which provides logo usage rules and downloadable JPG/vector formats: https://www1.szu.edu.cn/images/szu_logo/
  - `shenzhen-university-emblem.png` is a crop of the standalone round emblem shown on the official logo specification sheet.
- Guangdong Mechanical & Electrical Polytechnic: cropped from the white school mark in the official school homepage masthead image (`images/logo.png`): https://www.gdmec.edu.cn/images/logo.png

The school marks remain their respective schools’ property.
